# LAUNCH_KIT.md

## Positioning
One-line pitch: AI Video App For Small Shops That Generates Product Showcase turns rough app ideas into AI-buildable project kits for vibe coding agents.

## Demo Script
1. Open the builder at /.
2. Enter: AI video app for small shops that generates product showcase videos from product photos and descriptions.
3. Fill target users: Small shop owners, local retailers, Shopee/TikTok Shop sellers.
4. Fill desired output: 30-second product showcase videos with text overlays, background music, and transitions.
5. Generate the kit.
6. Open Repo & Tool Map and show URL-only references.
7. Open Task Plan and copy the first coding-agent task.
8. Export Codex Pack or ZIP.

## Beta Audience
- Non-coders who want AI to build a first MVP.
- Solo builders who need structure before opening Codex, Cline, Cursor, or Claude Code.
- Agencies and freelancers who turn client ideas into implementation plans.

## Launch Checklist
